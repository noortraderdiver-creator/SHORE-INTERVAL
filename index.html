Join Surface Interval

Membership is limited to certified divers. Upload a photo of your cert card — a real person reviews every application before you can book or host.

No booking fee, ever. As a member you pay the nightly rate plus any extras the host sets (like a cleaning or tank-fill fee) — always shown before you book.

You'll get "pending" status right away. Booking and hosting unlock once a reviewer approves your cert.

Host application

This is separate from joining as a member — you're already verified. Now tell us about the place itself: photos, location, and exactly what it costs to stay.

Surface Interval takes 12% of each booking's nightly total. No listing fee, no monthly charge. Add your own extra fees below (cleaning, tank-fill, etc.) — those go straight to you.
